What these numbers mean
Order
An instruction to buy or sell. An order might never execute.
Fill
Some or all of an order has actually executed on the exchange.
Volume capture
The share of qualifying public market volume our orders received. Different from the fraction of our own order filled.
Drawdown
A decline from a previous cumulative profit peak. It helps describe the losing stretches.
